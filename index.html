How to Play

How many Taylor Swift songs can you name?

  • Type a song title into the guess box and press Enter or the Guess button.
  • Correct guesses will automatically appear in their album!
  • Some songs appear in multiple albums or categories. One guess can fill multiple spots.
  • All song titles with 4 or more words can be entered using acronyms (e.g., ATWTMV for All Too Well (10 Minute Version)).
  • Punctuation and symbols (commas, periods, apostrophes, question marks, etc.) are optional.
  • Featured artists do not need to be included in your guesses.

Buttons

  • Guess: Submit a song title.
  • Give Up: End the quiz, reveal missing songs, and see your results.
  • Reset: Start over and erase your progress.
  • Share Results: Share your final score with friends.

Your progress is automatically saved in your browser.
